Frequently asked questions

What you are wondering.

Management

Is it as good as the big AI services in the cloud?

Yes. The language model on the box is on a par with the leading cloud services for the work an office does: reading, summarising, writing, translating, calculating and reasoning. Try it yourself, with your own questions.

What does it actually cost?

AI Box S costs SEK 49,000 and AI Box M SEK 89,000, excluding VAT, once. Installation, walkthrough, updates for at least five years and a three-year warranty are included. No fees per user, per question or per word. If you would rather pay monthly, you can lease for 12, 24 or 36 months. The longest lease gives the lowest monthly cost: SEK 1,950 for S and SEK 3,500 for M over 36 months.

Why not just buy a cloud service?

If you have no sensitive data and only use AI now and then, a cloud service can be cheaper. The box is for those whose data may not leave the building, or where many people use AI every day. The box costs the same however much you use it, while a cloud service charges per user or per question.

What happens if the box breaks?

During the warranty, the first three years, we repair or replace it on your premises.

Which AI model does it run?

An open language model, run by our own software. When better models arrive, you get them as an update.

How soon can we have it?

Within two weeks of ordering it is installed on your premises, wherever you are in Sweden.

Staff

How do I use it?

In the app on your phone (iPhone and Android) or in the browser on your computer. Each device is paired with the box once, with a code. After that it is a chat like any other.

Does it understand Swedish?

Yes, both written and spoken. You can write and dictate in Swedish and get answers in Swedish, or in another language.

Can I upload documents?

Yes. PDF and Word files and other text files, straight into the chat. Follow-up questions about the same document are answered without reading it again.

Does it work from home?

Yes. At the office the traffic goes straight to the box. From home it is encrypted all the way from the app to the box: directly when possible, otherwise through a relay server that cannot read the content.

Can I trust the answers?

Treat what it writes as a draft from a capable colleague: read it through before you use it. It makes no decisions and gives no diagnoses.

IT

What does the box need?

Power and a network cable. It is a tower workstation that stands on the floor or in a server rack. No screen is needed.

Which ports and what traffic?

No inbound ports and no port forwarding. The box gets its address by DHCP and only opens outbound encrypted connections: for remote access through the tunnel, for updates when you install them and for web search if you have it switched on. None of your documents or conversations are sent out.

Can our own software use it?

Yes. The box has OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs, so most programs and AI tools that can talk to a cloud service can talk to the box instead. If you need a particular connection to your system, we help with it.

How is it updated?

With signed updates that you install when it suits you, or automatically if you switch that on yourselves. Updates are included for at least five years. We keep working on making the AI faster and better, and those improvements reach your box with the updates: the one you buy today gets better over time.

How do we control who can reach it?

Only devices paired with the box can reach it. You decide which, and can remove a device at any time.

Can you reach the box remotely?

Only if you let us in. If you need help, you switch on maintenance mode on the box yourselves and approve our connection. Otherwise we cannot reach it, and support happens on your premises.

Data protection

Does any data leave the box?

No, no content. Documents, questions, answers and history are processed and stored on the box. With web search on, only the search terms themselves go out, and every search shows in the chat. You can switch web search off entirely.

Do we need a processor agreement with you?

Not for the AI. It runs on your own box, and there is no cloud provider or sub-processor in the chain. During support, on site or remotely once you have switched on maintenance mode, we may see data on the box. For that we offer a data processing agreement (DPA) that covers the support only.

What about confidentiality and patient data?

The data does not leave the building and is not handed to any vendor, so when staff use the AI nothing is disclosed to a third party. Access is controlled by which devices you have paired with the box. The box does not replace your patient record system; the record is kept there as today, under the Swedish Patient Data Act.

Can we delete data?

Yes. Everything is on the box, so what is deleted there is gone. There is no copy with us or in any cloud.
