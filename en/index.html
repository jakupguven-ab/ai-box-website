What you're wondering.

Is it as good as the big AI services?

Yes, for the work an office does: reading, summarising, writing, translating and reasoning, in Swedish and English. The best proof is your own, which is why we start with sixty days on your own documents.

We already have Microsoft 365. Do we need this?

Microsoft 365 carries on as today. The box is for what you would rather not put in someone else's cloud, and it costs the same however much you use it.

How do staff use it?

In the app on their phone (iPhone and Android) or in the browser on their computer. It's a chat like any other, and you can dictate. From home, traffic is encrypted all the way to the box.

Do we need a processing agreement?

Not for the AI, since there is no provider in the chain. During on-site support, or remote support when you have switched on maintenance mode yourselves, we may see data on the box. For that we offer a processing agreement that covers the support only.

What does our IT need?

Power and a network cable. The box stands on the floor or in a network cabinet. No inbound ports: it only opens outbound encrypted connections, for apps that reach it from home, for updates when you install them and for web search if you switch it on. It has OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible APIs for your own programs.

What if it breaks?

During the three warranty years we repair or replace it on site.

Which AI model does it run?

An open language model run with our own software, tuned to the machine. When better models arrive you get them as an update.
